Paper 1: Theory 40%

⏱ 1h 30m📊 80 Marks📝 Written

Sections 1–10: systems, devices, storage, networks, effects of IT, applications, life cycle, safety & security, audience, communication.

⏱ 2h 15m📊 70 Marks💻 Practical

Documents (17), Databases (18), Presentations (19) with core skills from Sections 11–16.

Paper 3: Practical B 30%

⏱ 2h 15m📊 70 Marks💻 Practical

Spreadsheets (20) and Website Authoring (21) with core management and editing skills.
