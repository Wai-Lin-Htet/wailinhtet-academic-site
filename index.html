Why Study CS & ICT?

Technology shapes every aspect of modern life. Studying Computer Science goes beyond syntax; it builds structured problem-solving, logical discipline, and systems-level thinking — empowering students to transition from passive consumers of technology to active creators.

The Importance of AI

AI is rapidly redefining research, industry, and education. Understanding AI is essential not just for building software applications, but for navigating modern economies. Integrating AI critically and ethically ensures we prepare forward-thinking, adaptable learners.
