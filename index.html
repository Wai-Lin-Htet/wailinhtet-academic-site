Current Research

Ph.D. Research

In progress

Students' Adoption of Artificial Intelligence in Secondary Education in Myanmar: A Management Framework for Equitable Learning Outcomes

This study aims to develop and validate a localized management framework for the equitable integration of AI in Myanmar's secondary education sector. Rather than treating AI as an isolated policy challenge, the framework extends existing ICT governance structures. The research looks at how students and teachers are affected by AI, building on the ICT, online teaching, and LMS practices already used in Myanmar education.

It combines the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), specifically Perceived Usefulness, Perceived Ease of Use, and Behavioral Intention, with Self-Regulated Learning (SRL) theory inside an Input-Process-Output (IPO) conceptual framework.
