    Exam Preparation — Edexcel International GCSE ICT (4IT0)

    Command words, technique tips, and a revision checklist to work through before the exam.

    Command words & how many marks they're worth

    WordWhat it asks you to doTypical marks
    State / Give / NameRecall one fact — no explanation needed.1 mark per point
    IdentifyPick out a key detail from the question or a resource.1 mark
    DefineGive a precise meaning of a term.1 mark
    DescribeGive an account of what something is or how it works.2–4 marks
    ExplainState a point and give a reason — look for "because"/"so that".2–4 marks
    CompareGive both similarities and differences between things.levels-marked
    DiscussExplore more than one side of an issue, then reach a conclusion.8 marks
    JustifyGive reasons that support a decision or opinion.levels-marked

    Technique tips

    • Match your answer's length to the mark allocation — one clear point per mark for "state" questions.
    • For "Explain", always add the reasoning: what happens and why/what the effect is.
    • For "Discuss", cover a benefit and a drawback, then finish with a short conclusion.
    • Use precise ICT vocabulary from the notebook (e.g. "bandwidth", not "internet speed").
    • Read the number of marks before you answer — it tells you how many separate points are needed.
    • Don't repeat the question back — spend every line on new, creditable information.
