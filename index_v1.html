Why Study Computer Science & ICT?

Technology shapes every aspect of modern life. Studying Computer Science and ICT goes beyond learning how to code; it builds foundational logical thinking, structured problem-solving, and the resilience to adapt to new systems. It empowers students to transition from passive consumers of technology to active creators and innovators.

The Importance of Artificial Intelligence

AI is rapidly redefining how we work, research, and learn. Understanding AI is essential not just for building better software applications, but for navigating the future economy. Incorporating AI tools securely and ethically into educational frameworks ensures we are preparing adaptable, forward-thinking learners.
