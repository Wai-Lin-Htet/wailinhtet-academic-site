Study on your own — no class needed

You can learn this course by yourself, at your own pace. Live online classes run Mon–Fri evenings and Sat–Sun (see the calendar on the course home page), but every section is written so you can study it alone. Follow these 5 steps for every section:

  1. Read the chapter notes.
  2. Try the exam questions without looking at the answers.
  3. Check the model answers and fix your mistakes.
  4. Mark complete on the course home page (sign in first) — your progress is saved.
  5. Test yourself with a past paper from the Past Papers tab.

Suggested pace: 1 section a week — 21 sections in about 21 weeks, finishing around 11 April 2027, with about 3 weeks left for past papers and revision before the May/June 2027 exams.
